# SDS-001 — Design Principles

## Principles

1. **One platform, many products** — shared language across all Sengiala surfaces.
2. **Human-centred** — serve real cooperative, operational and community needs.
3. **Clear before clever** — prioritise comprehension and task completion.
4. **Accessible by default** — design for varied abilities, devices and environments.
5. **Trustworthy** — make status, consequences and system feedback visible.
6. **Mobile-first and responsive** — support field and office use.
7. **Offline-aware** — communicate connectivity and synchronisation states clearly.
8. **Consistent but adaptable** — preserve shared semantics while respecting platform conventions.
